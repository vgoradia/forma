# Forma — 6-month path to an acquirable B2C asset

**Goal:** Build a real consumer business with metrics a buyer can diligence, then pursue a **lump-sum exit** (acqui-hire, strategic buy, or micro-PE rollup).

**You own 100%** until you raise or sell — keep it that way for a clean sale.

---

## What buyers actually pay for

| Asset | Why it matters | How we prove it |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Engaged users** | Distribution | Vercel Analytics: MAU, `scan_completed`, retention |
| **Revenue** | Multiple on cash | Stripe MRR (Plus) + Amazon affiliate dashboard |
| **Brand + SEO** | Cheap traffic | Branded search, `/scan` landing, shared verdict links |
| **Product** | Speed to market | Live app, domain, stack docs, clean repo |
| **Legal hygiene** | No deal friction | Privacy, Terms, affiliate disclosure |

Acquirers rarely pay “AI magic” premiums for solo apps. They pay for **traffic + revenue + optionality** in a category they already play in (shopping, fashion, affiliate, consumer AI).

## 6-month roadmap

### Month 1 — Launch & baseline
- Ship launch video + 1–2 social channels (see `LAUNCH.md`)
- Affiliate tag live; track `scan_completed`, `share_analysis`, `affiliate_click`, `plus_checkout_started`
- **Targets:** 500+ visits, 100+ scans, 10+ shares, first affiliate clicks

### Month 2 — Prove the loop
- Double down on whatever channel drives **scans** (not likes)
- A/B hooks in video; one new creative per week
- **Targets:** 2k+ visits, 300+ scans, 5+ Plus trials or 1+ paid sub

### Month 3 — Revenue story
- Forma Plus positioning stable; mention MRR in founder posts
- Optional: 2–3 micro-influencers (UTM per creator)
- **Targets:** Documented MRR or affiliate $ (even $50–200/mo is a start)

### Month 4 — Retention & identity
- Ship **signed-in scan history** (Supabase) — buyers want real accounts, not only localStorage
- Email list or waitlist for “price drop on saved verdicts”
- **Targets:** 100+ Google sign-ins, 20%+ week-2 return scan rate

### Month 5 — SEO & defensibility
- Lightweight pages: “[brand] dupe”, “is [item] worth it” (only high-quality)
- Press / Product Hunt / founder podcast if metrics support it
- **Targets:** Organic traffic trend up; 5k+ monthly visits aspirational

### Month 6 — Package & outreach
- **Data room** (folder): analytics screenshots, Stripe export, affiliate reports, stack diagram, user funnel
- **Buyer list** (10–20 names): shopping extensions, fashion media, affiliate networks, consumer AI labs
- Outbound: short blurb + metrics + “open to strategic acquisition”
- **Targets:** 2–5 serious conversations; LOI range depends on MRR & growth (often **low six figures** for micro-B2C with proof, higher if MRR is real)
